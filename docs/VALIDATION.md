# Prototype validation and limitations

## Reported milestone

During development, the user-reported prototype was working with embedded sensing, Firebase synchronisation and a web dashboard. A two-hour chart and AP fallback were among the reported features.

## What this repository does *not* establish

- Calibrated measurement accuracy.
- Temperature accuracy under full operating conditions.
- Electrical safety or environmental qualification.
- Long-term continuous uptime or alert latency.
- Backup-power endurance.
- Compliance with clinical, laboratory or pharmaceutical storage standards.
- Production deployment or independently audited test results.

## Evidence that may be added later

Only after checking for confidential information and permission to publish:

1. Real photographs of the prototype with any sensitive identifiers removed.
2. Sanitised screenshots of the monitoring dashboard, labelled with capture date and whether sample data is used.
3. A short recorded demonstration of sensor response, door state and alarm transitions.
4. Reproducible non-proprietary code examples or test logs.
5. A validation plan specifying acceptance criteria rather than implying they have already been satisfied.

## Why disclosure matters

A working engineering prototype demonstrates valuable skills in firmware, cloud communication and interface design. It should not be confused with a certified or deployed medical monitoring system.
